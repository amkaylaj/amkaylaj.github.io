Bamboo Cathedral

Written August 2026

This is my neverending tunnel I don't know if there is light at the end, I haven't made it that far. I do know that light peers in, through cracks in the side, spaces between the boughs Nodes and internodes, towering as they bend their heads in reverence to one another. In reverence to the light that gives them life For me illusions, memories, also life. A bamboo cathedral. A tunnel of life. Light reflecting, then traveling Through the crystal, through time Shadows neither belonging to the Corporeal nature of the boughs, Nor to myself and all my being It belongs to the memories Images reflected Sounds, whispers dancing with the creaking of the sheaths Voices amassing guilt, fear, love, empathy The clink of a marble being pitched against another An ocean within those glass spheres And as they roll away The tide also recedes Sand sinks giving rise to the past There the marbles rest, ossified in the dunes The end and the beginning are akin to one another I already walk with it Shadows tangled with the light.
