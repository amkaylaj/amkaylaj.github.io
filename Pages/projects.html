UVA Venture Forward: PPG Sensor · Spring 2026

Designing a Wearable Pulse Oximeter and Heart Rate Monitor with Sensor Fusion

This project was a part of the UVA Venture Forward initiative which is a program to support students and their entrepreneurial endeavors in the School of Engineering and Applied Sciences. The goal of this project was to address some of the measurement disparities in SpO2 readings across skin tones, as well as improve signal quality by reducing motion artifacts with heart rate monitors. I was able to combine my knoledge in biology with applied skills in electrical engineering.
