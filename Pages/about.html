About me

I'm currently a 4th year undergraduate student receiving my Bachelors of Science in Biomedical Engineering along with a minor in Electrical Engineering. I have lived between Brooklyn, New York and Trinidad and Tobago for most of my life. I am really excited about applying engineering skills and knowledge to solving difficult problems. I've had the opportunity to explore this through undergraduate research in the UVA Biomedical Engineering Department at UVA with the Naegle Lab, as well as with Calyx Global by exploring the policies and impacts of the compliance carbon market in the voluntary carbon market space. My love of reading and English literature has continued to flourish throughout my life, which has lent itself to my love of poetry writing. I also have a great love for all things outdoors especially rock climbing. I'm currently reading Love in the Time of Cholera by Gabriel Garcia Marquez. Feel free to email me or send me a message on LinkedIn about potential opportunities, projects or research of interests, or if you would just like to chat.
